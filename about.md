# 🧭 About Me 🧭


I'm a security researcher and red teamer. I came in sideways, through locksmithing.

Locksmithing taught me how things open, and the locks kept pulling me further in. I started out as the person recommending a line of commercial access-control locks, and ended up reading credentials out of their flash, injecting their firmware, and getting them to lie about their own audit trail. That work is the [T2/T3 lock series](/projects/). In 2026 it became my first major main-stage talk, at ToorCamp, right after the keynote.

Then came the agents. I build teams of narrow AI agents, each with one job, along with the files they use to hand work to each other. [Swarnam](/building-swarnam.html), the red team swarm I built in three weeks, went with me to WRCCDC regionals and NCCDC nationals in my first year red teaming. The same approach runs a meal planner my household uses every week. The work I write about most now is the unglamorous half: the checks and gates around a system that never runs the same way twice.

I believe physical, digital, and social engineering are parts of a single threat model. That hasn't changed.

This blog is where the research lands. What worked, what didn't, and what I got wrong, with the corrections left on the record. Not everything here is production-ready. The goal is impact, not polish.

Off the keyboard, I play trombone. I have for most of my life.

Talks, workshops, and the rooms I've shown up in are on the [Talks](/talks/) page.
